# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** the owner/CEO of a small or mid-size business (≈10–200 people), Philippines first, then Southeast
  Asia; any industry (distribution, food, retail, services, manufacturing …). They set the company up, hire AI
  employees, approve their configurations and sensitive actions, and read daily reports. They are busy, not
  technical, and accountable for money and customers.
- **Also:** admins and managers who configure agents, review documents and business rules, record business records;
  members and viewers who ask the knowledge base and read reports. Roles are permissions, never job titles.

## Product Purpose
AI Business OS lets a company hire, configure, test, govern and audit AI employees (Finance, Sales, Operations,
Support, HR, Procurement, Marketing, Executive). The platform learns the business from its documents (RAG), proposes
job descriptions and tasks, tests agents in a sandbox before activation, runs approved work with recorded evidence,
and reports every day from those records. Success: the CEO trusts what the agents did because every claim traces to
evidence, and nothing sensitive happened without their approval.

## Positioning
Governance first: AI employees are hired and supervised like staff — approvals bound to exact configurations and
parameters, staged activation after simulation, write-once evidence ledgers, reports projected from records (never
model prose). Development adapters are always labelled; the UI never shows invented data.

## Operating Context
Onboarding wizard → document upload & review → business rules → team selection → configuration & approval →
readiness tests → activation → tasks & runs → approval inbox → daily reports, notifications, Control Center,
usage & costs → settings and data controls. Money is shown in the company's currency (often PHP); times in the
company time zone (often Asia/Manila).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-tenant: each workspace is one company; data never crosses workspaces.
- Next.js 16 + Tailwind v4 frontend, FastAPI backend; light theme with dark-mode tokens; WCAG 2.x AA enforced by an
  axe gate in Playwright on every journey page.
- Tenant branding (Phase 8): a workspace may upload its company logo; the app derives the workspace's accent colours
  from it and picks a type mood from a curated set by industry. Layout, status colours (success/warning/danger/info)
  and platform chrome stay the platform's, so governance signals read the same in every workspace. Derived colours
  must pass contrast checks; the platform falls back to its own accent when they can't.
- UI shows only real backend data; no fake stats, no controls without a working endpoint.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: **AI Business OS** (fixed). No existing logo, palette or typeface to preserve — the platform identity is
  being designed in Phase 8.
- Voice: plain, precise, calm; says what is and isn't verified ("Development mode", "Outcome unknown — not counted as
  done"); never hype.

## Evidence on Hand
No customers, testimonials, logos of clients, benchmarks or pricing exist. Marketing surfaces must not invent them.
Real product screenshots exist under `frontend/test-results/shots/` (generated by the E2E journeys).

## Product Principles
1. Evidence over assertion — every number and claim links to its source record.
2. The human approves; the agent proposes — sensitive actions wait for the CEO.
3. Honest state — unknown, unverified, dev-adapter and not-connected states are visible, never dressed up.
4. The company's business, not ours — workspaces wear the customer's brand; governance signals stay constant.
5. Calm control — dense operational information made legible, never alarming without cause.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA (axe gate in CI), keyboard operable, visible focus, colour never the only signal. English UI; Filipino
users on mixed device quality — must hold up at 390 px width and on modest hardware.
