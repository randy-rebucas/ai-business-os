## Direction contract

THESIS: Agent output is presented like an audited annual report — report-scale figures with numbered notes that open
their evidence. Refuses the category default of identical white KPI cards with an indigo accent and pill badges.

OWN-WORLD: Deep report green shell (#0f3d36) with a brass rule; cool paper ground (#f4f6f5), white sheets, ink
#121a18; heavy 3px ink rules over figure rows and table heads instead of card borders; tracked small-caps eyebrows;
grotesk display numerals (Schibsted Grotesk 800, tabular) over a workhorse text face (Public Sans); square-ish
corners (2–4px); brass note keys (#8a6210). Tenant accent drives actions, links, focus, active nav marker.

STORY: The owner opens Control Center, reads three figures at report scale, follows a note key to the runs or
approvals that produced it, approves the one held action, and leaves trusting the numbers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left green rail with platform mark, the workspace's own logo and name, grouped nav with a brass-marked
active item; page eyebrow (period · time zone), H1, a figure row ruled in ink (completed, awaiting approval, spend vs
budget with a capacity bar), each figure with a superscript note key; below, runs table with glyph+word states; a
Notes list keyed 1…n linking to evidence.

FORM: Annual-report identity program — candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 2d3b5e2a. Raises: budgets as
fill against a hard capacity (j-card); states carry glyph + word, not hue alone (emission rail); setup shows only the
next decision (wayfinding); approval inbox focuses one held action, the next queued (vertical feed).
